Frequently asked

Questions, answered simply.

If your requirement is different, send a free enquiry and describe it.

Can I ask for customization before paying?

Yes. Select the relevant services and send the ₹0 enquiry. Your enquiry gets an ID and can be reviewed before a final scope is agreed.

Can I select more than one service?

The current checkout allows up to two main service packages plus optional add-ons.

How is payment secured?

Razorpay handles checkout. The server verifies the Razorpay signature, fetches the payment/order, checks capture status and verifies the amount before recording the payment as captured.

Will I get a receipt?

For a successful payment, SRIJAN generates a PDF receipt and can email it when the configured email provider is working.

Do I need an account?

You can use the enquiry/checkout flow without logging in. Creating an account gives you access to your client dashboard and connected project history.

Can I use Google login?

Yes, once Google is enabled as an OAuth provider in your Supabase Authentication settings.

Can I add pages, images or integrations?

Yes. The service catalog includes optional add-ons such as extra pages, image batches, WhatsApp, Maps, forms, basic SEO, APIs and practical automation.
